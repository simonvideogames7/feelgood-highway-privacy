Privacy Policy – Feelgood Highway

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Feelgood Highway (the "App") is a retro racing game published by Simon Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The App does not request any device permissions.

Data stored on your device

The App saves your game progress, such as best scores, settings and cleared worlds, locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

Internet use

The App works fully offline and does not connect to the internet or to any servers.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The App contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.
